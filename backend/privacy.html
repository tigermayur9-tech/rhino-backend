Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rhino ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and services (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information we collect

Location data. Rhino is a ride-hailing service. To match riders with nearby drivers and to provide live trip tracking, we collect your device's precise location (GPS) while you use the Service:

  • Riders: your location is used to set your pickup point and to let your driver find you.
  • Drivers: your location is used while you are online to match you with ride requests and to show your live position to the rider during a trip.

Account information. When account features are enabled, we may collect your name, email address or phone number, and profile photo.

Trip information. We collect ride details such as pickup and drop-off locations, fare amounts, and timestamps to provide trip history and receipts.

Device information. We may collect device type, operating system, and app version to keep the Service working smoothly.

2. How we use your information

  • To connect riders with drivers and complete ride requests
  • To calculate fares and provide receipts
  • To show live trip progress
  • To improve the Service and provide customer support
  • To meet legal and safety obligations

3. How we share your information

  • Between riders and drivers: during an active trip, each party sees the other's name and live location so the trip can be completed safely.
  • Service providers: we may share data with providers who host our servers or process payments on our behalf.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information where required by law or to protect the safety of users and the public.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. How long we keep your information

We keep trip records and account data for as long as needed to operate the Service and comply with legal obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

5. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for location collection at any time (via your device settings or by stopping use of the Service)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: support@rhinoapp.app

6. Children's privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified through the app or by updating the date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at: support@rhinoapp.app
